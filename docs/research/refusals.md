# Refusals — every refusal a real run met, counted

**FROZEN on 2026-10-14, by `yield-benchmark`'s Slice 3.** This is a snapshot and is kept as it was read: from
`data/cutroom.sqlite`, read-only, over the 52 runs of 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-26, written down at commit `e7e6f2d`
on 2026-09-26 — the commit where session 55's last six runs were added. **What the runs did is now
`docs/research/benchmark.md`**, written by `scripts/benchmark.mjs` and never typed by hand: three live documents
counting the same refusals is the drift `CLAUDE.md` refuses for the decisions, so this one stopped counting rather
than becoming a second authority. Nothing here is recounted, and nothing here is corrected — where it and the
ledger disagree, the ledger is the authority. One disagreement is already recorded in
`docs/features/yield-benchmark.md`: Gate 1's clipping reasons are **45 strings on 43 pictures**, and the count
below is of the 43 pictures, not of the strings.

The owner asked in session 53 (2026-09-25) for every reason a picture, a clip or a cut was refused to be noted, so the
engine can be fixed from it. This file counts them. It was generated from the database, read-only, over **all 52 real
runs** (2026-09-21 to 2026-09-26; session 55 added six), from `picture.gate1`, `take.verdicts`, `render.gate7`, `degradation`, `lost_call`,
`refused_answer` and each failed or stopped run's `result`. The ledger at the end is every refusal, as the gate wrote
it. What each pitfall was and what was done about it stays in `docs/reel-pitfalls.md`; a fix to the engine is a
feature with its gates.

- **Pictures: 127 judged at Gate 1, 67 refused — 53%.** 43 of the 67 carried _"clipped by the edge of the frame"_, and
  **26 were refused for clipping alone**. By role: canonical 34 refused of 58, derived 14 of 22, standalone 19 of 47.
  Lettering is second: **24 pictures**, 21 of them in one reel of screens and hardware (P-017), some for marks the
  witness itself called illegible (P-023).

  once (P-011), "sit well beside" once (P-015).
- **The witness marked 295 of the 899 things it listed as clipped by the edge** — one in three. Only a named subject's

## What would remove the most, for the features that fix them

Candidates, not choices — each is the owner's, with a feature's gates. Never by weakening a gate (CLAUDE.md rule 3).

1. **Clipping at Gate 1** — 45 of 94 picture reasons, 8 of the 15 degraded shots, 4 run endings. The picture is asked for
   with nothing about its framing, and a 9:16 frame cuts anything tall, long or many. Candidates: the picture prompt
   asks for the whole subject with space around it; the brief rules (compact, small, from a distance) become a check a
   brief is read against before it is admitted.
2. **Every shot opening on a picture** — image-to-video takes were refused 4 times in 46 (one shot held on its
   picture, one passed on its second take), and text-to-video takes 28 times in 71, stopped six reels at their third take
   (P-003; P-008, P-017 and P-018 twice each) and drifted out of the reel's world once (P-015). Pictures cost 5¢ to 8¢; `upToThree` (S-150) is the rule today.
3. **One request ending a run** — a network error on a picture's status or file (P-013), or on any call while this
   machine's DNS stalls (P-020, four runs in one session), a witness answer that is not
   JSON (P-014), fal's 504 (P-009): look again, ask again, and `resume-failed` (S-157).
4. **Subjects made of many things** — a cluster, a swarm, the stars, the pyramids of Giza: counted as one or as a
   hundred, and refused by the count rule twelve times (six pictures, six clips).
